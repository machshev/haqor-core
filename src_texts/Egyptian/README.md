# Egyptian and Coptic dictionaries

For tracing the Hebrew Bible's Egyptian loanwords (ADR 7). Fetched as
published by `scripts/fetch-egyptian-lexicons.sh`, at the commits `SOURCE`
records.

## `aed-dictionary.xml`

The list of Egyptian words of the AED (Ancient Egyptian Dictionary) TEI corpus,
<https://github.com/JKatzwinkel/aed-tei>: more than 35,000 entries, each with
its TLA id (`tla…`), transliteration, part of speech, German and (for many)
English translations, and references (`Wb` is the *Wörterbuch der ägyptischen
Sprache*). Its source is the "Teilauszug der Datenbank des Vorhabens
'Strukturen und Transformationen des Wortschatzes der ägyptischen Sprache' vom
Januar 2018" (<https://nbn-resolving.org/urn:nbn:de:kobv:b4-opus4-29190>), the
Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae of the Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences.
Each lemma is online at `https://thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae.de/lemma/<id>`.

The repository states: "All files: CC-BY-SA 4.0"; the file's own header:
"Metadata and texts are released as Creative Commons, Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)".

## `coptic-lexicon.xml`

The Comprehensive Coptic Lexicon, version 1.2 (2020), of the Berlin-Brandenburg
Academy of Sciences' project "Strukturen und Transformationen des Wortschatzes
der ägyptischen Sprache" and the Freie Universität Berlin's Database and
Dictionary of Greek Loanwords in Coptic, as published by KELLIA
(<https://github.com/KELLIA/dictionary>, <https://coptic-dictionary.org/>).
Each entry has a TLA id and German, English and French glosses, with references
(`CD` is Crum's *Coptic Dictionary*).

The repository states: "Lexicon data licensed CC BY-SA 4.0"; the data is
published at DOI 10.17169/refubium-2333.
